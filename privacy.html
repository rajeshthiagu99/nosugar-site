NoSugar privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026 (deletion section expanded)

What stays on your phone

Your answers stay on your phone: your first name if you give one, your reasons for quitting sugar, where your sugar comes from and how much, when cravings hit, your daily check-ins, slips and streak. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If Android backup is on, Google may back up the app's data to your own Google account.

What is sent

To understand how the app is used and to measure advertising, the app can send usage events to Google Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Meta (Facebook) app events and RevenueCat (subscription status).

An event is a fixed name such as "onboarding_completed", "paywall_viewed" or "checkin_done", plus, for a purchase, the price and product. These services also receive device information they collect themselves, including the Android advertising ID, app version, device model and country. Because NoSugar is a quit-sugar app, an event by itself tells these companies that you use one.

We never send your answers, your name or anything you type.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not see your payment details.

Health information

NoSugar gives general information about added sugar. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you have diabetes or another medical condition, talk to your doctor before changing your diet.

Deleting your data

On your phone: open Android Settings › Apps › NoSugar › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall NoSugar. This deletes everything the app stored: your name, answers, streak log and settings. We hold no copy, because none of it is sent to us.

Usage events: the analytics services keep the events described above, linked only to a random install ID and your advertising ID, for their standard retention periods (Firebase up to 14 months, Mixpanel up to 5 years by default, Meta and RevenueCat per their own policies). To have them deleted, email the developer address shown on the NoSugar page on Google Play and include the Feedback ID from the app's help screen if you have one; we will ask each service to delete the data for that ID within 30 days. You can also reset your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads, which stops new events being linked to the old one.

Purchases: Google Play keeps your purchase records under its own policy. Cancel a subscription in Play Store › Subscriptions.

Contact

Use the developer email address shown on the NoSugar page on Google Play.

NoSugar privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026 (sign-in, friends and leaderboards)

What stays on your phone

Your answers stay on your phone: your reasons for quitting sugar, where your sugar comes from and how much, when cravings hit, your daily check-ins, slips and streak. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If Android backup is on, Google may back up the app's data to your own Google account. On iPhone, if iCloud Backup is on, Apple may include the app's data in your own iCloud backup.

Your account (sign-in and friends)

Version 1.0 of NoSugar has no account or sign-in: everything stays on your phone. From version 1.1, NoSugar asks you to sign in with Google (Android and iPhone) or Apple (iPhone) so you can add friends and see leaderboards. Our own server (api.runningtogether.fit, hosted by Hetzner in Germany) then stores only: the sign-in provider's account identifier (an opaque ID, not your email; we do not store your email address), the display name you choose (you can change it in Settings), your friend code and invite link, who you are friends with in the app, and two numbers: your current and best sugar-free streak. Your friends see your display name and streaks; if your display name is set, it can also appear on the global leaderboard. Your answers, sugar log, slips and cravings are never sent.

When someone opens your invite link, our server counts the click; it does not record who opened it. If you install from an invite, Google Play passes the friend code to the app so you two become friends; on iPhone the invite page copies the link, and the app reads the clipboard only if you tap "Have an invite code?" on the Friends screen.

What is sent

To understand how the app is used and to measure advertising, the app can send usage events to Google Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Meta (Facebook) app events and RevenueCat (subscription status). The iPhone app uses Mixpanel and Meta app events only.

On iPhone, the app first shows a short screen explaining this, and nothing is sent before you continue. Apple then asks whether NoSugar may track you. If you allow it, Meta also receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) so it can tell which ad brought you to the app. If you ask the app not to track, the IDFA is not shared and the app works the same; Meta and Mixpanel still receive the usage events below, without it. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

An event is a fixed name such as "onboarding_completed", "paywall_viewed" or "checkin_done", plus, for a purchase, the price and product. These services also receive device information they collect themselves, including the advertising ID (Android advertising ID, or the iPhone IDFA if you allowed tracking), app version, device model and country. Because NoSugar is a quit-sugar app, an event by itself tells these companies that you use one.

We never send your answers, your name or anything you type to these services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads, or turn off tracking on iPhone in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Links to other sites

The "Read & watch" section links to articles and videos on other sites (such as the NHS, WHO, Harvard, Cleveland Clinic, Mayo Clinic and YouTube). They open in your browser or an in-app browser sheet, and those sites' own privacy policies apply. NoSugar does not tell them anything about you.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play on Android and by Apple (the App Store) on iPhone. We do not see your payment details.

Health information

NoSugar gives general information about added sugar. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you have diabetes or another medical condition, talk to your doctor before changing your diet.

Deleting your data

Your account: open Settings › Delete account in the app. This deletes your account, display name, friend code, friendships and streak numbers from our server straight away. Signing out keeps the account; deleting it cannot be undone.

On your phone: on Android, open Settings › Apps › NoSugar › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall NoSugar. On iPhone, delete the app (touch and hold the icon › Remove App › Delete App). This deletes everything the app stored on the phone: your answers, streak log and settings. We hold no copy of these, because they are never sent to us. (Your account, if you have one, is deleted from Settings › Delete account.)

Usage events: the analytics services keep the events described above, linked only to a random install ID and your advertising ID, for their standard retention periods (Firebase up to 14 months, Mixpanel up to 5 years by default, Meta and RevenueCat per their own policies). To have them deleted, email swedhabhaga1612@gmail.com and include the Feedback ID from the app's help screen if you have one; we will ask each service to delete the data for that ID within 30 days. You can also reset your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads, which stops new events being linked to the old one.

Purchases: Google Play and Apple keep your purchase records under their own policies. Cancel a subscription in Play Store › Subscriptions on Android, or in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

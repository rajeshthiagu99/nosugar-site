NoSugar support

Help for the Android and iPhone apps

Restore NoSugar Plus

On iPhone: open NoSugar › the gear on the home screen › Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you paid with. On Android: Settings › Restore purchase, with the Google account you paid with.

Cancel or change your subscription

On iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › NoSugar Plus. On Android: Play Store › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Reminders are not showing

Check that notifications are allowed for NoSugar in your phone's Settings, and pick a check-in time in NoSugar › Settings. Reminders are part of NoSugar Plus.

Add the widget

On iPhone: touch and hold the home screen › Edit › Add Widget › NoSugar. On Android: touch and hold the home screen › Widgets › NoSugar.

Your data

Your answers and sugar log stay on your phone. See the privacy policy for what is sent and how to delete it.
